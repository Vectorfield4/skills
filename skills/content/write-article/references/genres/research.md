# research — исследовательские

## Case

A paper, model, dataset, benchmark, or collaboration. The reader sees a
measured result and can judge its reach.

## When chosen

There is a measurement, a method, and a limit worth recording. A manager sees
the stake, an engineer can repeat the run.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: охват. Reader: specialist and lead together. One action: open a related
solution or breakdown.

## Volume and structure

RU 900–1500 words. Structure: PSI, Breakdown or Journey
(`structures/psi.md`, `structures/breakdown.md`, `structures/journey.md`). A
benchmark or dataset gets a table or a diagram.

## Layout Topology & Rhythm

Paragraph blocks:

- **Anchor block (A).** 3–5 high-density technical or analytical sentences: a
  core proposition, a grounding metric, and a local friction point.
- **Breather block (B).** 1–2 sentences, each at most 12 words: a blunt verdict,
  an operational directive, or a hard contrast.
- **Cluster block (C).** 4–6 compound technical or narrative sentences for
  unbroken linear reasoning, a step-by-step analysis, or a trace teardown.

This genre's footprint:

| Target volume | Target reader | Allowed blocks | Wave cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 900–1500 | Analyst / tech-lead | A, B, C | Analytical wave: C -> A -> B (deep thesis to punchy takeaway) |

## Evidence

Numbers with conditions and a source. A limits section is mandatory: where the
method fails, on what sample, with what error. The result is stated as a fact
with context, never as an ad.

## Links

The service that produced the result, a solution it serves, and one adjacent
technical breakdown.

## Voice

Neutral and specific. «На выборке 2 400 кадров доля пропусков 0.4 %» beats
«высокая точность».

## Anti-examples

- A result with no sample size.
- A benchmark table without conditions.
- A conclusion that hides the failure cases.

## Spec asks

State `category: research`, the benchmark or dataset table format, and what to
do when only a partial measurement exists.
