# case-study — бизнес-кейсы

## Case

How a client uses a solution, with ROI and an interview. The reader sees a
number and the path that produced it, and trusts both.

## When chosen

A project has a before/after measurement. A case page exists to link to.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: решение. Reader: a buyer weighing the risk. One action: request form or
the case link.

## Volume and structure

RU 900–1300 words. Structure: PSI or Journey (`structures/psi.md`,
`structures/journey.md`). Journey's failed-attempts block carries the tradeoff;
a smooth path reads as a story, not a report.

## Layout Topology & Rhythm

Paragraph blocks:

- **Anchor block (A).** 3–5 high-density technical or analytical sentences: a
  core proposition, a grounding metric, and a local friction point.
- **Breather block (B).** 1–2 sentences, each at most 12 words: a blunt verdict,
  an operational directive, or a hard contrast.
- **Cluster block (C).** 4–6 compound technical or narrative sentences for
  unbroken linear reasoning, a step-by-step analysis, or a trace teardown.

This genre's footprint:

| Target volume | Target reader | Allowed blocks | Wave cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 900–1300 | Enterprise buyer | A, C | Journey cadence: C -> A -> C -> B (context to verified metric) |

## Evidence

A before/after metric with the measurement conditions. Write it as a value, a
delta, and a window: «сборка 7 минут вместо 12, быстрее на 42 %, замер за март
по 210 сборочным прогонам». «Мы сильно ускорили» is banned. Weak spots are
named. Direct client speech is quoted or its absence stated honestly.

## Links

The case entity, the solution, and the services used.

## Voice

Documentary. The client's words and the numbers carry the text.

## Anti-examples

- A metric with no measurement window.
- A smooth story with no tradeoff.
- A quote that restates the marketing line.

## Spec asks

State `category: case-study`, the metric and its conditions, and how to handle
a client who declines to be quoted.
