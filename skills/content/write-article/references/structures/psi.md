# PSI (problem → solution → effect)

## When chosen

A concrete problem with a buyer behind it, and a measured effect to show.
Genres: product, case-study, corporate, community, and research with an outcome.

## Skeleton

1. **Problem.** The reader's situation and what it costs today. Not a product
   gap, the reader's gap.
2. **Solution.** What was built and how it works: the mechanism, the
   integration, the contract.
3. **Effect.** The measured change with its window, then the one action.

## Blocks

- Problem: one screen, a number that sizes the cost.
- Solution: the mechanism, plus at least one trade-off.
- Effect: value, delta, window, and a low-threshold CTA.

## Failure modes

- Problem framed as the vendor's sales problem.
- Solution with no trade-off.
- Effect with no measurement window.
