# How-To (sequential)

## When chosen

There is a procedure the reader can reproduce and reach a working result.
Genres: technical and product.

## Skeleton

1. **Problem.** What the reader wants to run or fix, in one sentence.
2. **Prerequisites.** Versions, access, data, the exact starting state.
3. **Steps.** Atomic, in order, one action per step, each with its check or
   expected output.
4. **Verification.** How the reader knows it works.
5. **Conclusion and CTA.**

## Blocks

- Prerequisites: no step may depend on something not listed.
- Steps: a step that cannot be checked is two steps.
- Verification: name the command and the expected result.

## Failure modes

- A step with no check.
- A skipped failure path.
- A copy-paste block with no parameter explanation.
