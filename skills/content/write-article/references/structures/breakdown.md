# Breakdown (context → mechanism → limits)

## When chosen

The reader needs to understand how something works, not to buy. Genres:
technical, media, research, and industry used as an explainer.

## Skeleton

1. **Context.** Why this is worth understanding now, in one paragraph.
2. **Mechanism.** The how, split into three to five parts that do not overlap
   and together cover it. Three or four fit working memory.
3. **Limits.** Where it fails, at what boundary, with what error.

## Blocks

- Mechanism: each part names its check or its effect. Every screen carries an
  anchor: a list, a table, a code block, a diagram (`common.md`).
- Limits: mandatory, in the body, never a footnote.

## Failure modes

- Overlapping parts that repeat each other.
- A happy path with no limits.
- A mechanism pitched at the level of a marketing summary.
