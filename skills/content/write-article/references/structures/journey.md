# Journey (narrative)

## When chosen

A decision or a project has a turning point worth telling. Genres: case-study,
corporate, and a large research effort.

## Skeleton

1. **Status quo.** How things worked before, with the number that defined
   normal.
2. **Challenge.** What broke or changed scale.
3. **Failed attempts.** What the obvious approaches cost and why they did not
   hold.
4. **Insight.** The approach that worked, as a mechanism.
5. **Result.** The measured change with its window.
6. **Lessons.** What a reader would do differently.

## Blocks

- Failed attempts: engineering facts, not drama. They carry the credibility.
- Insight: still a mechanism with limits, not a reveal.
- Result: the same evidence rule as PSI, value plus delta plus window.

## Discipline

The arc is engineering, not emotion. No Pathos, no «путь», no triumph framing. A
Journey that fails `filters/NegativeConnotationTextFilter.md` or the anti-slop
filters is not a Journey, it is a press release.

## Failure modes

- A smooth path with no failed attempts.
- A turning point with no mechanism.
- Emotion standing in for a number.
